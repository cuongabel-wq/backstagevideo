Send your finished videos to your TikTok drafts — in bulk.

BackstageVideo is a desktop tool for creators who manage several of their own TikTok accounts. Pick the videos on your computer, pick your accounts, and BackstageVideo delivers each video to that account's TikTok inbox as a draft. You then open TikTok, add your caption, sound and settings, and post it yourself.

How it works

  1. Sign in with TikTok for each of your accounts (Login Kit). BackstageVideo only asks for user.info.basic and video.upload.
  2. Select video files from your computer and choose which accounts should receive them.
  3. BackstageVideo uploads each file through the official TikTok Content Posting API (upload to inbox / draft).
  4. Open the TikTok app, tap the inbox notification, edit and post. Nothing is ever posted publicly without you.

BackstageVideo runs entirely on your own computer. It has no server and does not collect your data. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

BackstageVideo is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.
