Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

BackstageVideo ("the App") is a desktop application that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Information the App handles

  • TikTok account information received through TikTok Login Kit with the user.info.basic scope: your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar URL. These are used only to show which accounts you have connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used only to upload videos to your account's drafts with the video.upload scope.
  • Video files you select. They are read from your computer and sent directly to TikTok's servers.

2. Where it is stored

All of the above is stored only in a local file on your own computer. The App has no backend server; the developer does not receive, collect or have access to your account information, tokens or videos.

3. How it is used

Information is used solely to deliver your videos to your TikTok inbox and to show upload status. It is not used for advertising, analytics or profiling, and it is not sold or shared with any third party other than TikTok, which receives your videos as part of the upload.

4. Third parties

Your videos and API requests are processed by TikTok under the TikTok Privacy Policy.

5. Your choices and deletion

You can remove a connected account in the App at any time, which deletes its stored tokens from your computer. You can also revoke the App's access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Uninstalling the App and deleting its data folder removes all locally stored information.

6. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
